AR & AP Analytics Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end Accounts Receivable (AR) and Accounts Payable (AP) analytics project designed to transform accounting transaction data into actionable financial insights, risk indicators, and executive management decisions.

The project combines accounting knowledge with Python, Pandas, Excel, financial risk analysis, and executive decision support.

🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze customer receivables and supplier payables.
- Measure outstanding balances and payment performance.
- Evaluate customer collection performance.
- Analyze supplier payment performance.
- Identify potential overpayment conditions.
- Verify customer receipts against invoices.
- Identify financial risk exposure.
- Provide executive-level financial KPIs.
- Generate management recommendations and prioritized actions.
- Support data-driven financial decision-making.

📊 Key Analysis Areas

💰 Accounts Receivable Analysis

The project analyzes:

- Total sales and receivables.
- Customer receipts.
- Outstanding receivables.
- Collection performance.
- Customer payment behavior.
- Receipt-to-invoice reconciliation.
- Potential overpayments.

💳 Accounts Payable Analysis

The project analyzes:

- Total purchases and payables.
- Supplier payments.
- Outstanding payables.
- Supplier payment performance.
- Supplier obligations and payment exposure.

⚠️ Aging and Risk Analysis

The project evaluates outstanding financial exposure and identifies areas requiring management attention.

Risk analysis includes:

- Outstanding receivables.
- Outstanding payables.
- Collection exposure.
- Payment exposure.
- Unverified receipts.
- Customer overpayment conditions.
- Financial control exceptions.

🧠 Executive Decision Support

The project converts analytical findings into management actions and recommendations.

Examples include:

- Prioritizing overdue customer collections.
- Reviewing high-risk receivable balances.
- Monitoring supplier payment obligations.
- Investigating customer overpayments.
- Improving receipt-to-invoice reconciliation.
- Strengthening financial control procedures.
